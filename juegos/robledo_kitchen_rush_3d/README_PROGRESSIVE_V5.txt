ROBLEDO KITCHEN RUSH 3D — PROGRESSIVE GROWTH V5

The restaurant now behaves as a persistent business rather than a fully equipped level.

STARTER LIMITS
- Compact Tier 1 floor.
- Maximum 2 dining tables.
- Maximum 2 active customer parties.
- Party size limited to 1–2 customers.
- Only Garden Salad and Rush Burger are unlocked.
- Maximum 2 active recipes.
- No fryer, oven, potato, cheese or dough supply at the beginning.

BUSINESS PROGRESSION
Cash and customer satisfaction persist locally between shifts. Fixtures are bought from business cash and the layout is saved. Expansion requires both money and satisfaction. Each tier increases physical floor size, table capacity, concurrent customers, party size, menu slots, ingredient-crate capacity and kitchen-equipment capacity.

FAST SERVICE LIFE RULE
The fast-service clock starts when a seated table finishes browsing and places its order. If the entire table is completed within 30 seconds, one lost life is restored, up to the normal maximum of three lives.

WALKOUT PENALTY
A customer walkout costs one life, reduces satisfaction by 10 percentage points and costs $12.

EXPANSION TIERS
1. Starter Bistro — 2 tables / 2 active parties / 2 menu slots.
2. Growing Café — 4 tables / 3 active parties / 3 menu slots.
3. Neighborhood Restaurant — 6 tables / 4 active parties / 4 menu slots.
4. Robledo Grand Bistro — 9 tables / 5 active parties / 5 menu slots.
